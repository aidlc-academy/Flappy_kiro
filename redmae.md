# Flappy Kiro

A fast-paced, browser-based Flappy Bird-style game featuring Ghosty. Navigate through the walls, build your score, and try to beat your best run.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a modern web browser, then select **Start Game**.

## Controls

- **Space** or **tap/click the game** — start, flap, or retry after a game over
- **P** or **Esc** — pause or resume during a game
- **Speed buttons** — choose Slow, Normal, or Fast; the speed can also be changed during play

## Files

- `index.html` — game page and controls
- `style.css` — visual styling
- `game.js` — gameplay, physics, rendering, and input handling
